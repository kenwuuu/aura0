Your sideboard is a pile on your playmat like any other. Cards imported under a
`Sideboard` or `Companion` header start there.

Point at any card and press `key:moveToSideboard` to send it to the sideboard;
open the pile and use `key:moveToHand` to bring one back.

The tile only appears once the sideboard holds something — most decks import
without one, and an empty tile would just fence off battlefield space. Moving a
card in brings it straight back.

Opponents see the count and nothing else, which is what a sideboard is in paper
Magic: you may know it holds fifteen cards, never which fifteen.
