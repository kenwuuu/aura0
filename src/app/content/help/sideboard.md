Your sideboard is a pile in the dock like any other. Cards imported under a
`Sideboard` or `Companion` header start there.

Point at any card and press `key:moveToSideboard` to send it to the sideboard;
open the pile and use `key:moveToHand` to bring one back.

Because it's a real zone rather than a list, it works for anything that plays
from outside the game — a companion, a wish target, or the pile you swap from
between games.
