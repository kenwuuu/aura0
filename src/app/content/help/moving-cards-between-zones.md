Point at a card and press a key, or right-click it and pick the move from its
menu. The same keys work from your hand, from the battlefield, and from inside
an open pile.

| Key | Sends the card to |
| --- | --- |
| `key:moveToHand` | your hand |
| `key:moveToDiscard` | your discard |
| `key:moveToExile` | exile |
| `key:moveToDeckTop` | the top of your deck |
| `key:moveToDeckBottom` | the bottom of your deck |
| `key:moveToSideboard` | your sideboard |

Dragging does the same thing: pull a card from your hand onto the board, or from
the board onto any pile in the dock at the bottom of the screen.

### Several at once

Select a group of cards on the battlefield and one press of a move key sends all
of them. See **Selecting several cards**.
