At the bottom of the action history panel there's a die picker and two buttons.

- Choose a die — **d4** through **d100** — and press **Roll**
- Press **Flip** for a coin

Results post to the action history, so everyone at the table sees the roll and
nobody has to be trusted to report it honestly.

Good for deciding who goes first, for Krark's Thumb, and for every card that
says "flip a coin".
