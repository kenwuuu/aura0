**Drag a card from your hand onto the board.** On a phone, press and hold the
card for a moment first, then drag.

Dragging works in every direction: hand to board, board to any pile on your
playmat, pile back to hand.

### Drawing

Press `key:draw` to draw a card. You don't have to be pointing at anything — it
always draws from your own deck.

If you'd rather click, right-click any empty part of the board (or tap it, on a
phone) and pick **Draw**.

### Most actions follow one rule

**Point at the thing, then press the key.** Hovering a card and pressing
`key:moveToDiscard` discards it; hovering a token and pressing `key:tokenDelete`
removes it. Every one of those keys also appears on the right-click menu of the
thing it applies to, so you never have to memorise any of them.
