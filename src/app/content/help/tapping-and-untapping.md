Point at a card on the battlefield and press `key:tap` to tap it. Press it again
to untap.

On a phone, tap the card to open its menu and pick **Tap**. (Tapping the same
card a second time swaps the menu for a zoomed preview, so you can read it.)

### Untapping everything

Press `key:untapAll` to untap every card you control at once — your usual
start-of-turn step. It also clears summoning sickness.

`key:untapAll` only touches your own cards, so it's safe to press at any time.
