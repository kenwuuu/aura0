Almost everything has a key. The **Shortcuts** tab at the top of this window
lists every one of them, grouped by where it applies — and it's generated from
the app itself, so it can't fall out of date.

### Two things worth knowing

**Point first.** Most keys act on whatever your cursor is over: a card in your
hand, a card on the battlefield, a pile, a counter. The same key does the right
thing in each place.

**You never have to memorise any of them.** Right-clicking anything shows its
menu with the keys printed beside each row, so you can learn them as you go.

### Search instead

Press **⌘K** (**Ctrl K** on Windows and Linux) to open the command palette. Type
what you want — *shuffle*, *mulligan*, *import* — and press Enter. It also
doubles as a searchable list of every shortcut.
