Click **Choose Deck** in the top left. You can paste a link to a deck you have
already built, or paste a decklist.

### From a deck site

Paste the URL of a public deck from **Moxfield**, **Archidekt**, **EDHREC**,
**MTGGoldfish**, or **TappedOut**. The deck has to be public — a private or
deleted deck can't be read.

### From a decklist

One card per line, in either of these shapes:

1. `1 Mountain` — quantity and card name
2. `1 Steam Vents (RVR) 412` — quantity, name, set code, collector number

The second form pins the exact printing. Without it you get whichever printing
we find first.

Section headers are understood too. `Sideboard` and `Companion` cards load into
your sideboard pile; `Maybeboard`, `Wishlist` and `Tokens` are ignored.

### Saved decks

Decks you import are saved in this browser, and **Choose Deck** lists them — so
you can switch decks mid-game without pasting anything again.
