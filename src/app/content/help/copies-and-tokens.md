Point at a card on the battlefield and press `key:copy` to make a copy beside
it.

This works on **opponents' cards too**, and the copy belongs to you — which is
how you take control of something, or make a token copy of a creature you don't
own.

### Token cards

Cards bring their own tokens with them: play something that makes Treasures or
Soldiers and those tokens are fetched automatically. You never need to import
them.

To create one yourself, open **Create ▾** and pick **Token Card**, then search
for it by name.

For ability markers — flying, deathtouch, vigilance — see **Counters and keyword
markers**.
