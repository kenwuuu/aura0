Your hand is yours alone. Opponents see how many cards you're holding, never
which ones.

**Actions ▾ → Reveal Hand** flips that: while it's on, everyone can see your
hand, and the action history records that you revealed it. Pick it again to go
back to private.

Use it for a card that says to reveal your hand, for a Gitrog-style "show me you
discarded", or just to prove you're not holding the removal.

### Random discard

**Actions ▾ → Random Discard** bins one card from your hand at random, for the
effects that make somebody else choose.
