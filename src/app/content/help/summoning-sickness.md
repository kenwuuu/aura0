Point at a creature you just played and press `key:sick`. It tilts 45° — a
visual reminder to everyone at the table that it can't attack yet.

Pressing `key:untapAll` at the start of your turn straightens every tilted card
back up, along with untapping them.

It's a marker, not a rule: nothing stops you attacking with a tilted creature.
Aura moves cards around, and leaves the rules to the table.
