Point anywhere on the battlefield and press `key:addCounter` for a **+1/+1**
counter, or `key:removeCounter` for a **−1/−1** counter. It appears at your
cursor — drop it on the creature it belongs to.

### Adjusting a counter

Point at a counter already on the board:

- `key:tokenIncrement` raises its number
- `key:tokenDecrement` lowers it
- `key:tokenDelete` removes it

On a phone, tap the counter and use the **+1** / **−1** rows in its menu — on
desktop those are handled by clicking the counter's top or bottom half instead.

### Ability counters

**Create ▾ → Counter** opens a grid of keyword markers (flying, deathtouch,
lifelink and so on) that you drag onto the board. The empty-board right-click
menu has the same grid.
