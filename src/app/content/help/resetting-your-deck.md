**Actions ▾ → Reset Deck** puts everything back: every card from your hand,
battlefield, discard, exile and sideboard returns to your deck, the deck is
shuffled, your life goes back to its starting total, and you draw a fresh
opening hand.

It's how you start game two without reloading anything or making a new room.

This throws away the current game state and can't be undone, so it asks first.
It only affects **you** — your opponents' boards are untouched.
