Point at a card in your hand and press `key:flip`. It plays face down — morph,
manifest, foretell, or anything else that wants a hidden card.

`key:flip` also works on a card already on the battlefield, to turn it face down
or face up again.

### Checking what it is

Right-click your own face-down card and pick **Peek** to see its front. The
preview is local: nobody else's screen changes, and the card stays face down on
the board.

Peek only works on your own hidden cards — you can't peek at an opponent's.

### From a pile

Open a pile, right-click a card inside it, and pick **Play to board facedown** to
put it straight onto the battlefield hidden.
