Most problems at the table are one of these four.

### Everyone must be on the same link

Copy the link with **Copy Game Link** rather than retyping the address bar. A
link without the `?room=…` part on the end drops you into a different game, and
you'll each be sitting at an empty table wondering where the other one went.

### Check the connection dot

The dot in the top bar says whether you're **Connected**. If it's red, click it
to open the network settings. A refresh reconnects you and your board comes
back — your game lives in your browser, so reloading doesn't lose it.

### Cards or piles look wrong

Refresh the page. If something still looks off after that, it's a bug worth
telling us about — grab the version number from **Settings → About** and bring
it to Discord.

### Deck import didn't work

The most common cause is a deck that isn't public on the site you copied it
from. Check that you can open the link in a private browser window; if you
can't, neither can we.
