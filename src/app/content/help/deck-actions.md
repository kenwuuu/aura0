Everything you do to your library lives in one place: **right-click your deck**,
or open **Actions ▾** in the game toolbar. Most of these have no keyboard
shortcut, so the menu is where to find them.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Draw** (`key:draw`) | Draw one card |
| **Draw X** | Draw a number of cards you pick |
| **Scry** | Look at the top cards, then reorder them or send them to the bottom |
| **Surveil** | Look at the top cards, then keep them or bin them |
| **Mill** | Put a number of cards from the top into your discard |
| **Play to board** (`key:playToBattlefield`) | Put the top card straight onto the battlefield |
| **Exile Top** | Exile the top card |
| **Shuffle** (`key:shuffle`) | Shuffle your deck |
| **Mulligan** (`key:mulligan`) | Shuffle back and draw a fresh hand |
| **View Deck** | Open the deck and look through it |

Scry and Surveil open the cards in a small tray where you decide each one's fate
before it goes anywhere — nobody else sees them.
