**Create ▾ → Label** drops a piece of text onto the board. Right-clicking an
empty part of the board offers the same thing.

Use them for whatever the cards can't say on their own: marking the monarch,
naming a card for Meddling Mage, tracking an emblem, writing down what a
Clue is attached to, or just labelling whose corner of the table is whose.

Labels sit on the battlefield like any other object — drag them around, and
delete them the same way you delete a counter.
