**Hold Shift and drag a box** across the battlefield to select everything inside
it.

To add or remove one card at a time, hold **⌘** (**Ctrl** on Windows and Linux)
and click it. On a Mac use ⌘ rather than Ctrl — macOS turns Ctrl-click into a
right-click.

### What a selection does

- **Dragging** any card in the selection moves the whole group together.
- **Pressing an action key** applies it to every card in the group. Tap, flip,
  discard, exile, delete — one keypress for all of them.

Click any empty part of the board to clear the selection.
