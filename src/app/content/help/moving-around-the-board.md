**Drag any empty part of the board** to pan around it, and use the **+** / **−**
buttons in the bottom-left corner to zoom. (On a phone they're in the top-right,
and pinching works too.)

Each player gets their own playmat, so the table grows as friends arrive — pan
over to see what an opponent has in play.

### Snapping

Cards snap to the background grid while you drag, which keeps the board tidy
when several people are moving things at once.

Hold **Alt** during a drag to place a card freely instead. If you'd rather that
be the default, turn off **Always snap to grid** in Settings → Gameplay — then
Alt does the snapping.
