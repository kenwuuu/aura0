The gear button in the bottom-left corner of the board opens Settings (top-right
on a phone).

**Profile** — your display name and player colour. Both are what opponents see
on your health box and cursor, so set them before a game with strangers.

**Display** — how big cards are in your hand, and how big the hover preview is.

**Gameplay** — whether cards always snap to the grid while dragging, and whether
deleting a card asks first.

**About** — replay the tour, and the version number. Include that version when
you report a bug; it tells us exactly which build you were on.
