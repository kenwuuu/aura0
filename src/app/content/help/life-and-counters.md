Point at any player's life total and press `key:gainHealth` to add a life or
`key:loseHealth` to remove one. This works on opponents too — handy when
somebody's hands are full of real cards.

For a bigger swing, **click the number and type the new total**, then press
Enter.

### Custom counters

Hover a player's life box and a **+** appears on its left. Click it to add a
counter with its own name and icon — commander damage, poison, experience,
energy, the monarch, or whatever your table needs.

Once added, click a counter's halves to raise or lower it, and remove it when
the game moves on.
