Point at any player's life total and press `key:gainHealth` to add a life or
`key:loseHealth` to remove one. This works on opponents too — handy when
somebody's hands are full of real cards.

The **−** and **+** beside the number do the same thing with the mouse, and for
a bigger swing you can **click the number and type the new total**, then press
Enter.

### Custom counters

Hover a player's life box and a strip appears beside it, ending in a **+**.
Click that to add a counter with its own name and icon — commander damage,
poison, experience, energy, the monarch, or whatever your table needs.

Each counter gets the same **−** / **+** controls, plus an **×** to remove it
when the game moves on.
