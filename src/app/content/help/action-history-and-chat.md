Every move anyone makes lands in the **Action Log** — the panel in the top-left
corner. Draws, discards, exiles, reveals, dice rolls and turn passes, in order,
with who did what.

It's what you check when somebody asks "wait, where did that go?".

### Chat

The box at the bottom of the panel posts a message into the same log, so table
talk and game actions read as one thread.

### Moving it out of the way

Drag the panel's header to reposition it, and use the chevron in that header to
collapse it to a bar. On a phone it lives in the HUD stack instead of floating.
