Every move anyone makes lands in the **action history** — the panel on the left.
Draws, discards, exiles, reveals, dice, and turn passes, in order, with who did
what.

It's what you check when somebody asks "wait, where did that go?".

### Chat

The box at the bottom of the panel posts a message into the same history, so
table talk and game actions read as one thread.

### Moving it out of the way

Drag the panel's header to reposition it, and use the chevron in that header to
collapse it down to a bar. On a phone it lives in the HUD stack instead of
floating.
