Press **Pass** in the game toolbar to tell the table your turn is over. It posts
to the Action Log so everyone sees whose turn it is now.

Aura doesn't enforce turns — nothing locks while somebody else is playing, and
you can move your cards at any time. Pass is an announcement, which is what a
game of Commander with four people talking over each other actually needs.

Your own start-of-turn housekeeping is `key:untapAll` (untap everything and
clear summoning sickness) followed by `key:draw`.
