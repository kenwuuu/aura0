Put a **Commander** header above your commander in the decklist. Cards under it
are dealt into your opening hand instead of being shuffled into your deck.

```
Commander
1 Mabel, Heir to Cragflame

Deck
1 Sol Ring
1 Plains
...
```

`Commanders` and `Command Zone` work as headers too.

### Partners and backgrounds

Two cards under the header works — but only if the list *says where the command
zone ends*, with a blank line or a `Deck` header after them. A text list carries
no card types, so that boundary is the only evidence that a second legendary is a
partner rather than the first card of your deck. Without it we keep the first
card as your commander and read the rest as deck, which costs you one drag
instead of putting a card you never chose into your opening hand.

### Mulligans

Your commander stays in your hand through a mulligan — everything else goes back
and you draw a fresh hand around it.
