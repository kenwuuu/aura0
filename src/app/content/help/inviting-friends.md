Click **Copy Game Link** in the top right and send the link to your friends.
Anyone who opens it joins the same table — there is no account to make and
nothing to install.

Everyone needs the *same* link, so copy it rather than retyping the address bar.

The whole app works on a phone, so a friend who is away from their desk can
still sit down at the table.

### Starting over

**New Game** (also top right) gives you a fresh, empty table on a new link. Your
old game keeps running for anyone still in it.
